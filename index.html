Before the camera turns on

  • What it reads: your breathing, from how your chest moves, and your pulse, from your face.
  • What is kept: nothing. Pendo does not record or save video or readings. They are used while you play and are gone when you stop.
  • Who does the reading: the Presage SmartSpectra SDK, built into this app. It connects to Presage over the internet to check the app's key. Pendo has switched its usage telemetry off.
  • You are in control: the camera only starts when you press the button below. "Stop and go back" turns it off at any time.
  • This is a game, not a health tool. The readings are rough. Don't use them to judge your health.
  • Breathe gently. If you feel dizzy, stop and breathe normally.

Your heart is a pendulum

Why a pendulum?

The story goes that in 1583 a young Galileo watched a lamp swinging in Pisa cathedral and timed it against his own pulse. Around 1602 the doctor Santorio turned the idea round: he lengthened a weighted cord until its swing matched a patient's pulse, then read the pulse off as a length. You have just done the same thing with a camera.

The formula is L = g·T² / 4π², where T is the time for one full swing.
